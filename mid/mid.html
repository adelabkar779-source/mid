Palestine Explorer

Palestine is a historic region in the Middle East made up of two separate areas the West Bank and the Gaza Strip

Where is Palestine?

West Bank: A piece of land that sits next to Israel and Jordan
Gaza Strip: A small, narrow piece of land along the coast next to Israel and Egypt
Capital: Palestinians claim Jerusalem (also called Al-Quds) as their capital. Ramallah is where their main government offices are today.
